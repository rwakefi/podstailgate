Arkansas vs. AUBURN

The Arkansas Razorbacks and the Auburn Tigers first clashed on the national stage in the 1984 Liberty Bowl, where Arkansas fell 21–15, planting the first seed of what would later become an SEC rivalry.

From the Arkansas perspective, the rivalry’s highs are unforgettable. In 2008, the Hogs shocked a ranked Auburn squad 25–22 in Fayetteville — a defining win in Bobby Petrino’s early tenure that reminded the league that Arkansas could punch above its weight. But the crown jewel came in 2015, when Arkansas survived a wild five-overtime slugfest, winning 54–46 in one of the most thrilling games in program history. These moments are the ones Hog fans hang banners around — proof the program can meet SEC contenders head-on.

For Arkansas fans, this rivalry isn’t just another Saturday — it’s a litmus test, a culture check, and probably a coach defining game.
