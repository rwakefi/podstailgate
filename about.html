Who We Are

A litmus test, a culture check, a great Saturday

Every home game we set up in the Razorback Gardens, directly in front of the Bathroom Pavilion, and cook a menu built for that week's opponent. Friends, family, first-timers walking by — everyone's welcome.

For Arkansas fans this rivalry stuff isn't just another Saturday. It's a reason to show up early, eat well, and be loud. Bring an appetite and a "Woo Pig."
